3. Critical path analysis

The critical (longest) path runs through Phase 2 Railway Track: Gate 3 (IFC approval, set by the 104-day Railway Track and 109-day Site Works Detailed Designs) → Phase 2 prequalification → submittal → PO → 245-day manufacture → delivery → receiving → track installation → tie-in → trackside signalling integration → dynamic test → trial running → SAR safety assessment → Taking-Over. Signalling & Telecom (about 104 days of float) and Turnouts (about 107 days) finish early and wait for the same dynamic integration test.

6. Cost loading, performance flow & cash flow

Two P6 cost resources: PERFORMANCE (progress value spread over every activity) and CASH FLOW (net invoiced cash, loaded on every activity on the invoicing basis plus the advance milestone). Both total SAR 99,999,000; P6 adds the two resources; set the price of one to 0 to exclude it from the budget, or read cost per resource. Click a month for the breakdown.

Observations

  • Cash runs ahead of performance by up to SAR 9.9 M (Nov-2026) because of the advance, PO and approval invoicing; performance leads cash by up to SAR 5.6 M (Feb-2028).
  • Peak monthly gross invoice SAR 12.9 M in Nov-2027.
  • Net cash first 6 months SAR 14.73 M, first 12 months SAR 33.74 M.
  • Advance recovery is proportional (10% of each certificate).
