How the numbers are built

Understanding these five rules explains almost everything in the workbook.

1
Every item is split into stages. Each procurement-and-construction item (for example the Main Building) becomes four lines: a1 vendor prequalification → a2 PO issuance → a3 manufacture & delivery → b installation & construction.
2
Cost is shared between materials and construction using the discipline split (e.g. Civil 20% materials / 80% construction). The materials share is then split 5% / 15% / 80% across the three procurement stages.
3
Design works are weighted by stage (concept, preliminary, detailed) and each stage into submission 40% and approval 60%. The raw stage weights add up to 105%, so they are normalised to keep the design total at SAR 4,889,000.
4
Gates drive the dates. Access permits (month 1) → concept → preliminary → detailed design → work permit (14 days) → construction. Phase 1 procurement starts 15 days after preliminary-design approval; Phase 2 only after detailed-design approval. Construction starts at the later of the work permit or its own delivery.
5
Cost is loaded over time. Preliminaries, design and procurement are spread evenly over their months; installation & construction uses a triangular ramp-up / peak / ramp-down. The monthly totals add up to the S-curve.
Heads-up from the workbook: the work-permit gate is the binding constraint for several buildings, so installation windows at the end of the programme are tight (IT, fence, gate and FF&E are only 2–4 months). Shortening the front-end approvals would relieve it – and you can test that directly in the Gantt.

Using the interactive presentation

Built for a management meeting – everything is clickable, and everything recalculates when you change a date.

Change the plan

  • Project start date (top bar, default 17 Oct 2026): the whole project moves with it.
  • Any activity’s start / finish: type a date in the Gantt, drag a bar, or open the activity and use the +/− week and month buttons.
  • Linking decides whether dependent activities move automatically, only get pushed, or stay put.
  • Weightages on the Assumptions slide: change a percentage and amounts and cash flow recalculate (the total stays fixed).
  • Edit text (top bar): click any dashed text – titles, explanations, insights – and type. Live values such as dates and amounts stay up to date; your wording is saved with the scenario.
  • The scenario summary lists every change against the Excel baseline and can export a CSV or a share link.

Present it

  • Full screen / Exit full screen, Next and Back, and Print (all slides, or save as PDF) are in the top bar.
  • Keyboard: ← → to move, F for full screen, Esc to close details.
  • Filters under the top bar: phase, EPC category, WBS group, long-lead, critical path, changed, and search.
  • Click any bar, slice, tile, row or month to open a details panel with dates, predecessors, successors and monthly spend.

Take it offline

Download the presentation as one HTML file. Double-click it and it opens in any modern browser – no internet, no installation, and all the interactive features keep working (editing dates, Gantt, S-curve, print).
